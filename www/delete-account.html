Delete Your AdisChat Account

You can delete your AdisChat account directly inside the app. Open Profile > Settings > Account > Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps.

What is deleted

Profile info, account data, linked devices, and push tokens are deleted or revoked.

Deleted or revoked

  • Profile information associated with your account
  • Account status and active session access
  • Linked desktop devices
  • Push notification tokens

May be retained

  • Legal, fraud-prevention, and security logs when required
  • Transaction records if required by law or payment rules
  • Messages already delivered to other users for conversation integrity

If you cannot access the app, submit a deletion request here or email contact@adischat.com.
